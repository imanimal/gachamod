# Glossary

The words each translation uses for gachamod's own things, so every screen says the same thing. Add a line when you
pick a word for something new.

## Voice

- zh-CN: 你, not 您. Short and plain, like a normal Windows app. Chinese punctuation (，。：？) in Chinese text, no
  space between Chinese and Latin words is fine either way but be consistent: put a space around Latin words and
  numbers ("已启用 3 个 Mod").
- es: Latin American Spanish, tú. "mod" and "skin" stay as gamers say them (los mods, las skins).
- pt-BR: você. "mod" and "skin" stay (os mods, as skins).
- ru: вы, lowercase. Infinitive or imperative on buttons the way Windows does ("Удалить", "Открыть папку").

A place named in a sentence ("Settings, Appearance", "Tools > Fixes"):

- zh-CN: “设置 > 外观”, with corner quotes (none inside parentheses).
- es and pt-BR: the English punctuation, "Configuración, Apariencia", "Ferramentas > Correções".
- ru: «Настройки > Оформление» in guillemets, "в разделе «Настройки > Оформление»".
- Breadcrumbs ("Settings › {page}") keep ›.

A setting, button or menu entry named in another line uses that label's translation word for word, in quotes in zh-CN
(“”) and ru («»): "Another skin already on", "Hide adult mods from the chatbot", "Use a controller in the menu",
"Not interested", "Show in Browse again", "Put back".
